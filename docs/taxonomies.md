# Taxonomies and terms

A **taxonomy** groups entries: categories, tags, topics. Its **terms** ("News", "Sports") have a title and slug in each language, and in a hierarchical taxonomy a parent ("Local" under "News").

## Setting up

Under **Structure → Taxonomies** (permissions `cms.taxonomies.view`, `.create`, `.update`, `.delete`), laid out like a collection's form (cards **General**, **Collections**, **Term pages**, **Index pages**): a title (and **Title in …** each other language, what visitors read on its term pages: `$taxonomy->titleIn($locale)`, `translations` in code), a handle (it can be changed later, with a warning: terms fields and templates that name it need the new one, roles keep their permissions), whether terms nest, and the **Collections** whose entries it groups. The link between a collection and a taxonomy is what gives entries terms: the entries of a collection it's attached to get its terms in the editor's **Taxonomies** card ([below](#giving-entries-terms)), and their list filters by it. Attaching never changes blueprints. It's made from either side: the taxonomy's **Collections** list, or the collection form's **Taxonomies** list ([collections](collections.md)); each attachment has **One term only** (`single`: an entry has one term of it at most, chosen from a list), set on either form (**One term only** under the collection in the taxonomy form's **Collections** card, **{taxonomy}: one term only** on the collection). Unticking a collection keeps its entries' terms of the taxonomy stored, but hides them everywhere (`$entry->terms`, `whereTerm()`, term pages, counts, the sitemap, the editor) until it's ticked again. The form's side card lists the collections it groups, each with one term only or several. In code: `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Taxonomies\AttachCollections::handle($taxonomy, $collectionIds, checkRoutes: true)` and `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Collections\AttachTaxonomies::handle($collection, [$taxonomyId => $single])` (both `@api`; they return the ids newly `attached` and `detached`, and refuse an attachment whose term or index pages would have a URL something else has). Deleting a taxonomy keeps its terms, out of sight: the list's **Trash** and **Restore** bring it back, and `sunrice:orphans --purge` deletes it permanently ([leftovers](structure.md#leftovers)).

Under **Taxonomies** in the sidebar, one item per taxonomy (`/admin/cms/taxonomies/{handle}/terms`; permissions `cms.terms.view`, `.create`, `.update`, `.delete`, or `cms.terms.{taxonomy}.{action}` for one taxonomy): add, edit, nest, reorder and trash a taxonomy's terms, in each language (see [Trash](#trash)). A term not yet translated shows the main language's title. Terms are dragged into order by their handle among the terms of their level, each taking the terms under it along. Clicking a term, or **New term** (`…/terms/create`, `?parent=` to start under one), opens one full term editor (`Cms/Terms/Edit`, as sunrice-cms's): title and slug in the language chosen, **Under** (its parent, never itself or a term under it), the taxonomy's fields (or the built-in description), SEO, **Page settings** (template), the language switch with which languages the term has (`term.translatedIn`), version conflicts and edit locks. A new term is created with all of it at once (`Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Terms\CreateTerm`, `@api`: `SaveTerm` then `SaveTermContent` in one transaction) and opens in its editor; `SaveTermContent` takes `parent_id` too. The old addresses (`/admin/cms/terms/{id}/edit`) redirect to the editor.

In code: `SaveTerm` (title, slug, locale, parent_id, version), `TrashTerm`, `RestoreTerm`, `DeleteTerm`, `MoveTerm` (one place up or down) and `ReorderTerms::handle($taxonomy, $ids, $parentId)` (the terms under `$parentId`, null for the top level: those given first in that order, the rest of the level after them as they were, numbered again in one transaction, logged as `reordered` and the site's cache cleared, unless the order was already so: it returns whether it changed; route `POST cms/terms/reorder` with `taxonomy`, `ids` and `parent_id`). Slugs are unique per taxonomy and language; left empty, they're made from the title. Each term translation has a `version`, counted up on each save of its title, slug or page content: `SaveTerm` and `SaveTermContent` given the `version` the editor opened refuse a save over someone else's newer one with an error on `version` (the term's page offers **Load theirs** or **Save mine anyway**, as for entries).

## Giving entries terms

An entry's terms are its own, shared by all its languages, for the taxonomies attached to its collection: the entry editor's **Taxonomies** card (under **Page settings**) has a picker per taxonomy, one term or several as the attachment says: the chosen terms show as chips (each with a ✕ to remove it, its parents in its tooltip, "News › Local", from the editor's deferred `termParents` prop, loaded just after the page, and `parents` on the picker's lookup by `ids`), and **Choose terms…** (**Choose a term…** for one term only) opens a searchable list of the taxonomy's terms by title, nested terms indented, chosen ones ticked. Type to search; ↑ and ↓ move, Enter picks, Esc closes and Backspace in an empty search removes the last chip. With several terms, picking one adds or removes it and the list stays open; with one, it replaces the term and closes. It's there whatever blueprint the entry has, or none, and hidden when the collection has no taxonomies. Only taxonomies whose terms the person may view (`cms.terms.view` or `cms.terms.{handle}.view`) are listed, and all of them to those who may change the entry's terms: who may edit it in every language (not translators) or create one. Taxonomies not listed keep their terms when the draft is saved. Their terms aren't shown to those people elsewhere either: **Compare with live**, a revision's **Compare** and summary, the **Changed** markers and `cms_get_entry` ([AI](ai.md)) cover the same taxonomies (`Sunrice\Cms\Support\Access::entryTaxonomies($user, $collection, $entry)`, `@api`).

Terms are the entry's, but each language is saved on its own: the editor sends only the taxonomies whose terms were changed in it, with the terms it loaded for them (`terms_base`), and a save over terms someone changed since from another language's editor is refused ("Someone else changed this entry's terms. Reload to see them."), so it never puts the old ones back. `SaveDraft` takes `terms_base` too, and `SaveEntryTerms::handle($entry, $terms, $base)`; without it, the terms given are saved as they are.

Terms follow the entry's draft and publish: saving the draft (any language) keeps the terms chosen as the entry's terms not published yet (`sunrice_entries.draft_terms`, term ids; null when they're the live ones), and publishing any of its languages makes them its live terms (`sunrice_entry_term`), for the attached taxonomies: at once, or, published for a date to come, when that date comes (`sunrice:publish-scheduled`, which the scheduler runs every minute; until then they stay pending, and the revision keeps them). Then the terms it was published with go live, those its revision kept, not terms chosen since, which stay pending for a publish of their own (unless another language published since, live already, put its own on the site); an entry or collection restored from the trash after that date puts them on the site then; terms of a taxonomy no longer attached are left alone. Until then the editor counts them as changes pending: the **Changed** marker on the card and each taxonomy, **Compare with live** (a row per taxonomy, the terms by title) and **Discard changes**, which goes back to the live terms. Each revision keeps the terms it was published with (`sunrice_revisions.terms`), so restoring it brings them back as terms not published yet, and its **Compare** shows them (a revision of the main language kept before revisions had terms brings back those its top-level terms fields held). Unpublishing or trashing an entry leaves its terms as they are. Translators (who may edit only some languages) never change the entry's terms: discarding their language's changes, restoring its revision or undoing a restore leaves the terms alone (`DiscardChanges` and `RestoreRevision` take `withTerms`, `true` by default).

In code: `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Entries\SaveEntryTerms::handle($entry, ['topics' => [3, 'news'], 'format' => null])` (`@api`; by taxonomy handle, term ids or slugs in any language, one, a list, or null for none; only attached taxonomies, one term at most with **One term only**, else a validation error on `terms.{handle}`; taxonomies left out keep theirs). `SaveDraft` and `CreateEntry` take the same as their input's `terms`, and `PublishEntry` publishes them. `$entry->draftTermIds()` and `$entry->liveTermIds()` give the ids, `$entry->hasUnpublishedTerms()` whether some aren't published.

The card doesn't carry the terms in the page: each picker loads them when it shows, from `GET cms/terms/picker?taxonomy=…` (route `sunrice.admin.cms.terms.picker`, for those who may view entries or terms or edit menus; like the entries picker, it serves a taxonomy only to those who may view its terms, `cms.terms.view` or `cms.terms.{handle}.view`, or when it's attached to a collection they may view, create or edit entries in, or a terms field in a blueprint of such a collection uses it, or, to those who may edit menus (`cms.menus.update`), when its terms have pages; else `403`), in tree order with their depth, or those whose title matches `q`, or some by `ids[]`: 100 at a time (only those are read with their titles, so a taxonomy of thousands of terms costs the same as a small one), the next ones from `offset` (`more` says whether there are), which the picker loads as the list scrolls to its end (or **Load more**); `except` (a term's id) leaves that term and the terms under it out. The editor sends the chosen terms' titles (`termLabels`; `TermsField::labels($ids)`).

Elsewhere the admin lists a taxonomy's terms in a plain select while it has at most 200 (`TermsField::LISTED`; `TermsField::big($taxonomyIds)` says which have more): the entries list's filter, a term's **Under** and a menu's **Term** link. A bigger taxonomy is searched with the same picker instead, so a page never carries thousands of terms: in the entries list's **Filters** menu (its taxonomy comes with `search: true` and no `terms`), in the term editor (`terms` null; the picker leaves out the term and the terms under it) and in the menu editor (`terms` null when the taxonomies with term pages have more than 200 terms between them: choose the taxonomy, from `termTaxonomies`, then search for the term).

What reads terms reads the live ones, of the taxonomies attached to the entry's collection (`Entry::terms()` and `Term::entries()` leave the others out): term pages, collections' term and index pages, the entries list's filter, `whereTerm()`, `<x-sunrice-cms::terms>`, the sitemap and menus. In templates, `$entry->terms` (an `EntryTranslation`, as entry pages and `Cms::entries()` give them) is the entry's terms, by taxonomy title, then in the editors' order (in a preview, those not published yet), and `$entry->termsOf('topics')` one taxonomy's; a list of entries reads them all at once:

```blade
@foreach ($entry->termsOf('topics') as $topic)
    <a href="{{ $topic->url() }}">{{ $topic->title() }}</a>
@endforeach
```

```php
$term->entries;                 // entries that have it (live terms)
$entry->entry->terms;           // an entry's live terms (an Entry's relation)
```

### Terms fields

A **Terms** field (with its taxonomy, and whether it takes several terms, and at most how many) is an ordinary field: a custom link to terms, like a "Featured topic", whatever the collection's taxonomies. Its values are stored with the entry's fields (shared by all languages) and are not the entry's terms: term pages, `whereTerm()` and the filters don't read them; `whereFieldTerm('featured_topic', …)` does. Templates get `Term` models (`$term->title()` and `$term->translation()->slug` in the page's language, falling back to the main one's, as `$term->url()`; `$term->title('id')` in another), or a collection of them with several, in the order chosen; deleted terms are left out. `TermsField::toAdmin()` sends the taxonomy's handle.

Sites made with 0.1 gave entries their terms through terms fields (attaching a taxonomy added one to the collection's blueprints): see [upgrading](../../../docs/upgrading.md).

## Term pages and fields

Terms have pages in two places:

- **In each collection** the taxonomy groups (under it in the **Collections** card): a page per term listing only that collection's live entries, at `{the collection's listing page URL}/{taxonomy handle}/{slug}` (`/books/tags/featured`; without a listing page, under the collection's handle; `_` in the handle becomes `-`), or the collection's own **term page URL** (and **… term page URL in …** for each other language). On by default for each collection ticked, off with its toggle. They're stored on the attachment: `Sunrice\Cms\Models\CollectionTaxonomy` (`@api`, the pivot of `$taxonomy->collections()` and `$collection->taxonomies()`) with `single`, `term_pages`, `term_route`, `term_routes` (`{"id": "/buku/tag/{slug}"}`), `pattern($collection, $taxonomy, $locale)` and `defaultPattern()`.
- **Shared** (**Shared term pages** in the **Term pages** card, off by default): a page per term listing its entries from every collection, at the **Shared term page URL** (`route`, like `/tags/{slug}`; left empty, `/{handle}/{slug}`; switched off, `null`). Worth it when several collections share the taxonomy.

`$term->url($locale)` on a `Term` (and `TermTranslation::url()`) links to the shared page, else the page in the collection shown (on an entry's or listing page), else the first collection's; `$term->url($locale, 'books')` (a collection or its handle) to the page in that collection, and `$translation->in($collection)->url()` likewise. No two of them share a URL pattern in a language: a collection's, a taxonomy's or a collection's term pages (checked when saving, naming the other: "Books · Tags"). A collection's term and index pages are checked with the URL they'll have, typed or by default, in every language, whenever they're switched on or changed, or the collection is ticked, and on import (so a collection `cinema` with the listing page `/films` can't take `/films/tags/{slug}` from a collection `films` without one); `AttachCollections` refuses a collection whose pages would clash, and `RouteConflicts::forAttachment()` says what clashes. `$taxonomy->hasTermPages()` says whether its terms have pages at all: shared ones, or in a collection.

Each term page lists its live entries, newest first, 10 a page; other languages under their prefix (`/id/topics/berita`), with each language's slug, or at their own **Shared term page URL in …** (`/id/topik/berita`; `routes`, `$taxonomy->routeIn($locale)`, [translated URLs](site.md#translated-urls)), the old addresses redirecting. A **Blueprint** gives terms fields of their own (a description, an image); without one, terms have a `description` (a textarea, `Taxonomy::DESCRIPTION`) to introduce their page, which the CMS's plain term page prints. Both, and SEO (title, description, share image, hide from search engines), are edited in each term's editor in the admin (`SaveTermContent` in code), per language, with the term's title and slug in that language (in a language it isn't translated into, giving it a title there adds it: `SaveTermContent` with `title`, and `slug`, renames or translates it through `SaveTerm` first, in the same transaction, checking `version` once); fields not translated come from the main language, inside groups, rows and blocks too ([shared fields](collections.md#languages)), and show read-only in the others (a term not in the main language yet has nothing to share: all its fields are its own). **SEO from fields** names the term fields that fill in what the SEO card leaves empty (see [SEO](site.md#seo)). A new slug on a term's page (when terms have pages) notes that the old address will stop working (404) unless a redirect is added: terms don't redirect by themselves (**Add a redirect** links to Structure → Redirects for those with `cms.redirects.create`; props `term.hasPage` and `redirectsUrl`). That counts collections' term pages, as the terms list's link to each term's page does; the editor's **How is it chosen?** lists each such collection's files first. A term may name its own template. Group fields, **Search and sharing** and **Page settings** fold away (remembered, opened on an error), and one person edits a term's page in a language at a time ([edit locks](../../admin/docs/locks.md), type `cms.term`).

The taxonomy's **Term page template** is the default for all its term pages, and a term's own **Template** overrides it for that term. Files a developer names for one term (`{slug}`) or for one collection's term pages come before the default, so a collection can have its own design for them ([defaults and overrides](site.md#defaults-and-overrides)). In full, term pages render with the first view that exists ([templates](site.md#templates)): the term's own template; on a collection's term page `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.{slug}` (`cms/collections/books/tags/featured.blade.php`) and `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.show`; then `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.{slug}` (the term's slug in the main language: `cms/taxonomies/tags/featured.blade.php`), the taxonomy's **Term page template** (`template`), `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.show`, `cms.taxonomies.show`, or the CMS's plain one, with [what term pages get](site.md#term-pages-get): `$term` (the `TermTranslation`), `$taxonomy`, `$collection` (null on a shared page), `$entries` (a paginator of its live entries, queried only when the template uses it), `$values`, `$richText` and the rest. In the head: `<x-sunrice-cms::seo :term="$term" />`. `$term->url($locale)` on a `Term` links to its page. Term pages are in the sitemap, but those hidden from search engines; a collection's only for terms with live entries in it.

Term pages and entries share URL patterns: the most specific matching pattern decides, and collections come before taxonomies when two are as specific.

## Index pages

An index page lists a taxonomy's terms, each linking to its page, with how many live entries it has. Like term pages, they're in two places:

- **In each collection** (**Index page** under it in the **Collections** card): the terms that collection's live entries have, counted there, each linking to its term page in that collection when the collection has term pages (else its usual page), at `{the collection's listing page URL}/{taxonomy handle}` (`/books/tags`; without a listing page, under the collection's handle; `_` becomes `-`), or the collection's own **index page URL** (and **… index page URL in …** for each other language). On by default for each collection ticked, off with its toggle; stored on the attachment (`CollectionTaxonomy`: `index_page`, `index_route`, `index_routes` like `{"id": "/buku/label"}`, `indexPath($collection, $taxonomy, $locale)` and `defaultIndexPath()`).
- **Shared** (**Shared index page** in the **Index pages** card, with **Index page blueprint** and **Index page template**, off by default): every term out of the trash, counted across collections, at the **Shared index page URL** (`index_route`, like `/tags`; left empty, `/{handle}` with `-` for `_`; switched off, `null`) and its own **Shared index page URL in …** per language (`routes` with `index_route`, `$taxonomy->routeIn($locale, 'index_route')`), the old addresses redirecting like listing pages'.

`$taxonomy->indexUrl($locale)` is the shared index page's URL, `$taxonomy->indexUrl($locale, $collection)` a collection's, null when off; `$taxonomy->hasIndexPage($collection = null)` says whether it's on. Index pages' URLs are fixed, like listing pages': none may be another listing page's or index page's in any language (checked when saving the taxonomy or collection and on import, naming the other: "Books · Tags"), and they're found before entries' and terms' patterns.

**Index page blueprint** (`index_blueprint`) gives index pages fields of their own (an intro, an image), filled in per page and language under **Content → Terms → ⋮ → Index page** (one item per page: **Index page**, **Index page: Books**; also linked from the taxonomy's form), with their SEO, behind the taxonomy's terms permissions (`cms.terms.view` to see, `.update` to save; route `sunrice.admin.cms.taxonomies.index-page.edit`, `?collection=books&locale=id`, and `.update`). A page switch picks the shared page or a collection's, and a language switch the language. Saved live, no drafts, with the same version check as listing pages; fields not translated are saved in the main language, inside groups, rows and blocks too ([shared fields](collections.md#languages)). They're stored in `sunrice_taxonomy_listings` (`Sunrice\Cms\Models\TaxonomyListing`, `@api`: `taxonomy_id`, `collection_id` null for the shared page, `locale`, `data` with the SEO card under `seo`, `version`); in code, `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Taxonomies\SaveIndexPage::handle($taxonomy, $collection, $data, $locale, $version, $seo)` (`@api`), and `$taxonomy->indexSchema()`, `$taxonomy->indexData($locale, $collection)`.

Index pages render with the first view that exists ([templates](site.md#templates)): the taxonomy's **Index page template** (`index_template`, for the shared page and every collection's; also set on the index page editor, **Template**), then in a collection `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.index` (`cms/collections/books/tags/index.blade.php`), then `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.index`, `cms.taxonomies.index`, or the CMS's plain one (the starter templates, `vendor:publish --tag=sunrice-cms-templates`, have a `taxonomies/index.blade.php` in the site's layout), with [what taxonomy index pages get](site.md#taxonomy-index-pages-get): `$taxonomy`, `$collection` (null on the shared one), `$terms` (`Term` models in their order, with `entries_count`), `$values`, `$richText`, `$title` (the taxonomy's title in the language; the SEO card's title is the page's `<title>`), `$url` and the rest. They're in the sitemap in every language with their alternates (but those hidden from search engines), have their own SEO (canonical, hreflang), structured data (a collection page; a collection's under its listing page in the breadcrumbs), body classes `page-taxonomy taxonomy-{handle}` (plus `collection-{handle}` on a collection's) and are kept by the [page cache](caching.md) like listing pages. Menus can link to them ([menus](menus.md)).

## Trash

Deleting a term moves it, and the terms under it, to the trash (`TrashTerm`): off the site, out of pickers and entries (an entry keeps it, and has it again once it's restored). In the terms list's **Trash**, restore it (`RestoreTerm`; under a parent still in the trash it goes to the top level) or delete it permanently (`DeleteTerm`; terms under it move up, entries lose it). A term other content points at (`Term::referencedBy()`, see [references](structure.md#references)) is named in the confirmation, and `DeleteTerm` refuses it unless `force` is passed.
