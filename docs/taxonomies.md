# Taxonomies and terms

A **taxonomy** groups entries: categories, tags, topics. Its **terms** ("News", "Sports") have a title and slug in each language, and in a hierarchical taxonomy a parent ("Local" under "News").

## Setting up

Under **Structure → Taxonomies** (permissions `cms.taxonomies.view`, `.create`, `.update`, `.delete`): a title (and **Title in …** each other language, what visitors read on its term pages: `$taxonomy->titleIn($locale)`, `translations` in code), a handle (it can be changed later, with a warning: terms fields and templates that name it need the new one, roles keep their permissions), whether terms nest, and the collections whose entries it groups (the entries list filters by it; a collection whose blueprints have a terms field for the taxonomy gets the filter anyway, so a terms field is enough). Ticking a collection gives each blueprint its entries may use a terms field for the taxonomy when it has none (at any depth, fieldsets included): labelled with the taxonomy's title, under its handle or a free variant (`topics_terms`), several terms allowed, saved like any blueprint change, so editors choose terms on its entries straight away. Unticking one keeps that field and what entries chose, unless **Also remove the terms field from collections you untick** (asked once the list changes) is on: then it goes from blueprints no ticked collection uses (values stay stored, out of sight). The form's side card lists the blueprints with a terms field for the taxonomy. In code: `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Taxonomies\AttachCollections::handle($taxonomy, $collectionIds, removeFields: false, checkRoutes: true)` (`@api`; it returns the blueprints given or relieved of a field and the collections newly `attached`, and with `checkRoutes` refuses one whose term or index pages would have a URL something else has), and `AttachCollections::blueprintsWithField($taxonomy)`. Deleting a taxonomy keeps its terms, out of sight: the list's **Trash** and **Restore** bring it back, and `sunrice:orphans --purge` deletes it permanently ([leftovers](structure.md#leftovers)).

Under **Taxonomies** in the sidebar, one item per taxonomy (`/admin/cms/taxonomies/{handle}/terms`; permissions `cms.terms.view`, `.create`, `.update`, `.delete`, or `cms.terms.{taxonomy}.{action}` for one taxonomy): add, edit, nest, reorder and trash a taxonomy's terms, in each language (see [Trash](#trash)). A term not yet translated shows the main language's title. Terms are dragged into order by their handle among the terms of their level, each taking the terms under it along. Clicking a term, or **New term** (`…/terms/create`, `?parent=` to start under one), opens one full term editor (`Cms/Terms/Edit`, as sunrice-cms's): title and slug in the language chosen, **Under** (its parent, never itself or a term under it), the taxonomy's fields (or the built-in description), SEO, **Page settings** (template), the language switch with which languages the term has (`term.translatedIn`), version conflicts and edit locks. A new term is created with all of it at once (`Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Terms\CreateTerm`, `@api`: `SaveTerm` then `SaveTermContent` in one transaction) and opens in its editor; `SaveTermContent` takes `parent_id` too. The old addresses (`/admin/cms/terms/{id}/edit`) redirect to the editor.

In code: `SaveTerm` (title, slug, locale, parent_id, version), `TrashTerm`, `RestoreTerm`, `DeleteTerm`, `MoveTerm` (one place up or down) and `ReorderTerms::handle($taxonomy, $ids, $parentId)` (the terms under `$parentId`, null for the top level: those given first in that order, the rest of the level after them as they were, numbered again in one transaction, logged as `reordered` and the site's cache cleared, unless the order was already so: it returns whether it changed; route `POST cms/terms/reorder` with `taxonomy`, `ids` and `parent_id`). Slugs are unique per taxonomy and language; left empty, they're made from the title. Each term translation has a `version`, counted up on each save of its title, slug or page content: `SaveTerm` and `SaveTermContent` given the `version` the editor opened refuse a save over someone else's newer one with an error on `version` (the term's page offers **Load theirs** or **Save mine anyway**, as for entries).

## Giving entries terms

Add a **Terms** field to the blueprint, with its taxonomy, and whether it takes several terms (and at most how many). Editors pick from the taxonomy's terms; only that taxonomy's terms are accepted. Terms fields are shared by all languages.

The editor doesn't carry the terms in the page: a terms field loads them when it shows, from `GET cms/terms/picker?taxonomy=…` (behind `cms.entries.view`; like the entries picker, it serves a taxonomy only to those who may view its terms, `cms.terms.view` or `cms.terms.{handle}.view`, or when a terms field in a blueprint of a collection they may view, create or edit entries in uses it, else `403`), in tree order with their depth, or those whose title matches `q`, or some by `ids[]`. A taxonomy of more than 100 terms is searched instead of listed whole. `TermsField::toAdmin()` sends the taxonomy's handle; the entry editor also sends the chosen terms' titles (`termLabels`; `TermsField::labels($ids)`).

Templates get `Term` models (`$term->title()`, `$term->title('id')`, `$term->translation()->slug`), or a collection of them with several, in the order chosen; deleted terms are left out.

When an entry is published, the terms of its top-level terms fields are kept in `sunrice_entry_term`, so queries can find "entries with this term" without reading JSON:

```php
$term->entries;                 // entries that have it
$entry->terms;                  // the terms of an entry
```

## Term pages and fields

Terms have pages in two places:

- **In each collection** the taxonomy groups (**Term pages in collections**): a page per term listing only that collection's live entries, at `{the collection's listing page URL}/{taxonomy handle}/{slug}` (`/books/tags/featured`; without a listing page, under the collection's handle; `_` in the handle becomes `-`), or the collection's own **term page URL** (and **… term page URL in …** for each other language). On by default for each collection ticked, off with its toggle. They're stored on the attachment: `Sunrice\Cms\Models\CollectionTaxonomy` (`@api`, the pivot of `$taxonomy->collections()` and `$collection->taxonomies()`) with `term_pages`, `term_route`, `term_routes` (`{"id": "/buku/tag/{slug}"}`), `pattern($collection, $taxonomy, $locale)` and `defaultPattern()`.
- **Shared** (**Shared term pages**, off by default): a page per term listing its entries from every collection, at the **Shared term page URL** (`route`, like `/tags/{slug}`; left empty, `/{handle}/{slug}`; switched off, `null`). Worth it when several collections share the taxonomy.

`$term->url($locale)` on a `Term` (and `TermTranslation::url()`) links to the shared page, else the page in the collection shown (on an entry's or listing page), else the first collection's; `$term->url($locale, 'books')` (a collection or its handle) to the page in that collection, and `$translation->in($collection)->url()` likewise. No two of them share a URL pattern in a language: a collection's, a taxonomy's or a collection's term pages (checked when saving, naming the other: "Books · Tags"). A collection's term and index pages are checked with the URL they'll have, typed or by default, in every language, whenever they're switched on or changed, or the collection is ticked, and on import (so a collection `cinema` with the listing page `/films` can't take `/films/tags/{slug}` from a collection `films` without one); `AttachCollections` refuses a collection whose pages would clash, and `RouteConflicts::forAttachment()` says what clashes. `$taxonomy->hasTermPages()` says whether its terms have pages at all: shared ones, or in a collection.

Each term page lists its live entries, newest first, 10 a page; other languages under their prefix (`/id/topics/berita`), with each language's slug, or at their own **Shared term page URL in …** (`/id/topik/berita`; `routes`, `$taxonomy->routeIn($locale)`, [translated URLs](site.md#translated-urls)), the old addresses redirecting. A **Term fields** blueprint gives terms fields of their own (a description, an image); without one, terms have a `description` (a textarea, `Taxonomy::DESCRIPTION`) to introduce their page, which the CMS's plain term page prints. Both, and SEO (title, description, share image, hide from search engines), are edited in each term's editor in the admin (`SaveTermContent` in code), per language, with the term's title and slug in that language (in a language it isn't translated into, giving it a title there adds it: `SaveTermContent` with `title`, and `slug`, renames or translates it through `SaveTerm` first, in the same transaction, checking `version` once); fields not translated come from the main language. **SEO from fields** names the term fields that fill in what the SEO card leaves empty (see [SEO](site.md#seo)). A new slug on a term's page (when terms have pages) notes that the old address will stop working (404) unless a redirect is added: terms don't redirect by themselves (**Add a redirect** links to Structure → Redirects for those with `cms.redirects.create`; props `term.hasPage` and `redirectsUrl`). That counts collections' term pages, as the terms list's link to each term's page does; the editor's **Which file?** lists each such collection's files first. A term may name its own template. Group fields, **Search and sharing** and **Page settings** fold away (remembered, opened on an error), and one person edits a term's page in a language at a time ([edit locks](../../admin/docs/locks.md), type `cms.term`).

Term pages render with the first view that exists ([templates](site.md#templates)): the term's own template; on a collection's term page `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.{slug}` (`cms/collections/books/tags/featured.blade.php`) and `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.show`; then `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.{slug}` (the term's slug in the main language: `cms/taxonomies/tags/featured.blade.php`), the taxonomy's **Term page template** (`template`), `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.show`, `cms.taxonomies.show`, or the CMS's plain one, with [what term pages get](site.md#term-pages-get): `$term` (the `TermTranslation`), `$taxonomy`, `$collection` (null on a shared page), `$entries` (a paginator of its live entries, queried only when the template uses it), `$values`, `$richText` and the rest. In the head: `<x-sunrice-cms::seo :term="$term" />`. `$term->url($locale)` on a `Term` links to its page. Term pages are in the sitemap, but those hidden from search engines; a collection's only for terms with live entries in it.

Term pages and entries share URL patterns: the most specific matching pattern decides, and collections come before taxonomies when two are as specific.

## Index pages

An index page lists a taxonomy's terms, each linking to its page, with how many live entries it has. Like term pages, they're in two places:

- **In each collection** (**Index page in …** under **Term pages in collections**): the terms that collection's live entries have, counted there, each linking to its term page in that collection when the collection has term pages (else its usual page), at `{the collection's listing page URL}/{taxonomy handle}` (`/books/tags`; without a listing page, under the collection's handle; `_` becomes `-`), or the collection's own **index page URL** (and **… index page URL in …** for each other language). On by default for each collection ticked, off with its toggle; stored on the attachment (`CollectionTaxonomy`: `index_page`, `index_route`, `index_routes` like `{"id": "/buku/label"}`, `indexPath($collection, $taxonomy, $locale)` and `defaultIndexPath()`).
- **Shared** (**Shared index page** under **Shared term pages**, off by default): every term out of the trash, counted across collections, at the **Shared index page URL** (`index_route`, like `/tags`; left empty, `/{handle}` with `-` for `_`; switched off, `null`) and its own **Shared index page URL in …** per language (`routes` with `index_route`, `$taxonomy->routeIn($locale, 'index_route')`), the old addresses redirecting like listing pages'.

`$taxonomy->indexUrl($locale)` is the shared index page's URL, `$taxonomy->indexUrl($locale, $collection)` a collection's, null when off; `$taxonomy->hasIndexPage($collection = null)` says whether it's on. Index pages' URLs are fixed, like listing pages': none may be another listing page's or index page's in any language (checked when saving the taxonomy or collection and on import, naming the other: "Books · Tags"), and they're found before entries' and terms' patterns.

**Index page fields** (`index_blueprint`) gives index pages fields of their own (an intro, an image), filled in per page and language under **Content → Terms → ⋮ → Index page** (one item per page: **Index page**, **Index page: Books**; also linked from the taxonomy's form), with their SEO, behind the taxonomy's terms permissions (`cms.terms.view` to see, `.update` to save; route `sunrice.admin.cms.taxonomies.index-page.edit`, `?collection=books&locale=id`, and `.update`). A page switch picks the shared page or a collection's, and a language switch the language. Saved live, no drafts, with the same version check as listing pages; fields not translated are saved in the main language. They're stored in `sunrice_taxonomy_listings` (`Sunrice\Cms\Models\TaxonomyListing`, `@api`: `taxonomy_id`, `collection_id` null for the shared page, `locale`, `data` with the SEO card under `seo`, `version`); in code, `Sunrice\Cms\Actions\Taxonomies\SaveIndexPage::handle($taxonomy, $collection, $data, $locale, $version, $seo)` (`@api`), and `$taxonomy->indexSchema()`, `$taxonomy->indexData($locale, $collection)`.

Index pages render with the first view that exists ([templates](site.md#templates)): the taxonomy's **Index page template** (`index_template`, for the shared page and every collection's), then in a collection `cms.collections.{collection}.{taxonomy}.index` (`cms/collections/books/tags/index.blade.php`), then `cms.taxonomies.{taxonomy}.index`, `cms.taxonomies.index`, or the CMS's plain one (the starter templates have a `taxonomies/index.blade.php`), with [what taxonomy index pages get](site.md#taxonomy-index-pages-get): `$taxonomy`, `$collection` (null on the shared one), `$terms` (`Term` models in their order, with `entries_count`), `$values`, `$richText`, `$title` (the taxonomy's title in the language; the SEO card's title is the page's `<title>`), `$url` and the rest. They're in the sitemap in every language with their alternates (but those hidden from search engines), have their own SEO (canonical, hreflang), structured data (a collection page; a collection's under its listing page in the breadcrumbs), body classes `page-taxonomy taxonomy-{handle}` (plus `collection-{handle}` on a collection's) and are kept by the [page cache](caching.md) like listing pages. Menus can link to them ([menus](menus.md)).

## Trash

Deleting a term moves it, and the terms under it, to the trash (`TrashTerm`): off the site, out of pickers and entries. In the terms list's **Trash**, restore it (`RestoreTerm`; under a parent still in the trash it goes to the top level) or delete it permanently (`DeleteTerm`; terms under it move up, entries lose it). A term other content points at (`Term::referencedBy()`, see [references](structure.md#references)) is named in the confirmation, and `DeleteTerm` refuses it unless `force` is passed.
