# Writing entries

Entries are the site's pages, posts and other content, kept in
**collections** (like *Blog* or *Pages*). Open **Entries** and pick a
collection.

## A new entry

Choose **New entry**, give it a title and fill in its fields. The address
(slug) follows the title until you change it. Fields marked with a star
are required.

## Drafts

Everything you save is a **draft**: the site keeps showing what was
published until you publish again. **Preview** shows the draft as the site
would.

## Finding things again

Search the list by title; each entry shows its status in every language.
Trashed entries can be restored from the **Trash**.
