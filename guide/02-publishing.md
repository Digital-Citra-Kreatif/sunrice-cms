# Publishing

**Publish** puts the draft on the site. Pick a date in the future to
schedule it: it appears by itself at that time.

## Changing something live

Edit and save: the change is a draft until you publish it again, so you
can work on it calmly. The editor says when the draft differs from what's
live.

## Going back

Each time you publish, the old version is kept. **Revisions** shows them;
restoring one makes it the draft again, to check and publish.

## Taking something down

**Unpublish** takes a language off the site and keeps its draft. **Trash**
takes the whole entry away, in every language, until it's restored.
