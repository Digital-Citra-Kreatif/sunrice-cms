# Forms and comments

## Submissions

What visitors send through the site's forms is in **Submissions**, newest
first. Open one to read it or download its files; export a form's
submissions as a spreadsheet. Old ones may be deleted automatically.

## Comments

New comments may wait for approval, depending on Settings → Comments.
In **Comments**, approve, answer, edit or mark them as spam. Answering
emails the commenter if they asked for it.
