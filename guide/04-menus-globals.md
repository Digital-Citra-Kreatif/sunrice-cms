# Menus and globals

## Menus

**Menus** are the site's navigation. Add links to entries, term pages,
listing pages or any address, move them up and down, and nest them up to
three levels. Changes are live when you save.

## Globals

**Globals** hold what the whole site shares: contact details, social
links, footer text. They're live when you save, so check before saving.
